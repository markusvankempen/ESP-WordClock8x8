❓ ESP-WordClock8x8 FAQ & Help

⚡ Quick Start & Hardware
What hardware & LED matrix is required? ▼

ESP-WordClock8x8 runs on standard 8×8 WS2812B (NeoPixel) LED matrices (64 RGB LEDs) paired with an ESP microcontroller.

BoardDefault GPIO PinBoard Label
ESP8266 / D1 MiniGPIO 4D2
ESP32 Dev ModuleGPIO 4 or 18D4 / G18
M5Stack Atom S3 LiteGPIO 8G8 (Header)
ESP32-C3 Super MiniGPIO 2IO2

Wiring: Connect Matrix 5V → ESP 5V / VIN, Matrix GND → ESP GND, Matrix DIN → ESP Data Pin.

What browsers support Web Serial Flashing? ▼

Web Serial is supported on desktop versions of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Opera (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS), plus Chrome for Android via USB-OTG cable.

Safari and Firefox do not currently support the Web Serial API.

🔧 Troubleshooting & Flashing
Why is my USB port or device not detected? ▼

1. Check your USB cable: Ensure it is a data USB cable and not a charge-only cable.
2. Install USB Serial Driver: Most ESP boards use a CH340G or CP2102 USB-to-UART bridge. If Windows/Mac does not detect the port, install the CH340 or Silicon Labs CP210x driver.
3. Close other apps: Ensure Arduino IDE, PlatformIO Serial Monitor, or 3D printer software (Cura/PrusaSlicer) are closed so they do not lock the COM port.
4. Bootloader Mode: On some ESP32-S3 / C3 boards, hold the BOOT button while plugging in USB to enter flashing mode.

Why is my screen dark or some pixels flickering? ▼

1. Check 5V Power: 64 WS2812 LEDs at full brightness require ~1A–1.5A peak. Use a quality 5V 2A USB power adapter.
2. Common Ground: Ensure the GND of the LED matrix is firmly connected to the GND pin of the ESP board.
3. Data Direction: Connect to DIN (Data In), not DOUT.

🔄 Orientation & Word Grid
The words are upside down or mirrored. How do I fix it? ▼

In Step 3 (Flip), click Rotate 180° to adjust your matrix orientation immediately. You can also adjust horizontal flip, vertical flip, and zigzag wiring at any time from the web settings at http://wordclock.local.

📶 Wi-Fi, Time Sync & Web UI
How does the clock set its time? ▼

Once connected to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, ESP-WordClock connects to NTP (Network Time Protocol) atomic time servers and syncs automatically with daylight saving time adjustments.

What if I change my Wi-Fi router or password? ▼

If WordClock cannot connect to its saved Wi-Fi for 60 seconds, it automatically creates a setup hotspot named WordClock-Setup. Connect your phone or laptop to this Wi-Fi and browse to http://192.168.4.1/ to enter your new credentials, or re-run this USB setup wizard anytime!

How do I customize word colors, brightness, and themes? ▼

Open http://wordclock.local in any web browser on your home network to access the full control panel with live word matrix preview, RGB color wheel, night dimming schedule, transition effects, and language options.
